# Code of conduct

jearless is a small open-source project. These are the expectations for
anyone taking part in it, maintainers included.

## The expectations

- **Be direct and be kind.** Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
- **Explain your reasoning.** In this project a decision is only as good as
  the argument behind it; a "no" with a reason is a contribution too.
- **Respect what has already been decided.** Read `manifest.md` before
  proposing a change to how the tool works — most refusals there carry the
  reason they were made, and a good proposal answers it.
- **Keep the space usable for everyone.** No harassment, slurs, personal
  attacks, or publishing another person's private information.

## Where it applies

Repository issues and pull requests, and any other space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer through GitHub (a private vulnerability report
under the **Security** tab works for this too). The maintainer may edit,
remove or decline a contribution, and may block participation when the
behaviour continues. Decisions are explained where they can be without
exposing the person who reported.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-specific policy, not the Contributor Covenant.
